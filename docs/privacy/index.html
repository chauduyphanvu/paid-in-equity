Privacy

Effective November 6, 2026. In short: the calculators collect nothing, and the newsletter keeps only what it needs to email you.

The calculators

Every calculator runs in your own browser. What you type stays on your device: no account, cookies, browser storage or analytics, and no request to any server once the page has loaded. Files you open in a calculator, such as a brokerage statement, are read on your device and never uploaded.

This site

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs each visitor's IP address for security, whether or not they use GitHub; GitHub's privacy statement covers that. Apart from that security log, the site sets no cookies, runs no tracking, and lets no other party collect information about what you do here or on other sites.

The newsletter

The newsletter is sent through Substack. When you subscribe, Substack collects your name and email address and gives them to us. Substack also shows us, for each subscriber, which emails they received and opened and which links they clicked, plus an activity rating based on things like email opens and web views. If you answer the optional sign-up survey (how you're paid and what you'd most like to read about), your answers are linked to your email address.

If you buy a paid subscription on the web, Stripe processes the payment; in Substack's iPhone app, Apple does. Substack's policy says it also collects billing details. Through Stripe, we can see a paying subscriber's name, email, billing address and country, and the card's brand and last four digits, but never the full card number.

Substack's own pages, where you subscribe, read and pay, are different: Substack says it uses cookies, some set by third parties, and may collect information about your activity after you leave its site. It doesn't support Do Not Track. Substack's privacy policy and Stripe's cover what they collect.

Who gets your information

Only the services that run the newsletter and this site: Substack (sign-up and email), Stripe or Apple (payments), GitHub (this site), the publisher's email provider, and the AI service that helps answer mail (below). We don't sell or rent it, we don't share it with sponsors, and your income, holdings and tax details never reach us.

Email you send us

The publisher reads your message. AI agents that help run the newsletter also read it and draft replies, so its text is processed by Anthropic's Claude service under Anthropic's terms. Please don't send account numbers, tax ID numbers or your own figures. Messages are kept in the publisher's email and a private folder, and used to answer them and fix errors. With your permission, and with anything that identifies you removed, a question may become the question in an issue. Your name never appears in public without your written permission.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe with the link at the foot of every email, or in your Substack settings.
  • See, correct or delete what we hold by writing to andzrev@pm.me or replying to any issue; we'll answer within 30 days. You can also delete your Substack account in your Substack settings.
  • Do Not Track: beyond GitHub's security log, this site and the calculators track no one, so there is nothing for it to switch off.

Changes

A changed policy goes on this page with a new effective date, and the next issue mentions any material change.
