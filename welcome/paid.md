**Subject:** Welcome to the paid edition of Paid in Equity

Thank you for joining the paid edition. Your membership pays for the research, the checking and the calculators, and keeps every free issue free. As in the free issues, AI agents research, write and check each paid issue, and every figure comes from a primary source.

## What you get

The paid edition is planned to open on March 30, 2027. From April, most topics get two Tuesdays: a free issue that answers the question completely, and a paid issue a week later that goes further. Help tied to a deadline stays free.

Each paid issue adds:

- **The full model**, walked through with its assumptions stated, as a spreadsheet attached to the post (the code stays public on [GitHub]({{github_url}}) for everyone)
- **Ten or more scenarios** for the four characters, Priya, Marcus, Dana and Leo, and the odd cases the free issue leaves out
- **A checklist of questions** to work through against your own numbers and plan documents

The paid calculators add what-if scenarios across several years, scenarios you can save as encrypted files on your own device, and a calendar of vesting dates and tax deadlines. Like the free ones, they run in your browser and send nothing anywhere.

## Where the downloads are

Each paid issue's spreadsheet is attached to its post on Substack, in the part for paid members. Open the post from the email, or find it in the [archive]({{substack_url}}/archive). The paid calculators are linked from the same posts.

## What doesn't change

Everything paid is still a calculator working on numbers you type in. Nobody tells you what to do with your shares, options or money. It's general information worked through fictional examples, not tax, investment or legal advice for your situation ([disclaimer]({{site_url}}/disclaimer/)).

Your membership renews automatically until you cancel. You can cancel at any time in your Substack settings, or in your Apple subscriptions if you subscribed in Substack's iPhone app.

Found a mistake? Reply to any issue. Confirmed errors are fixed in public in the [corrections log]({{site_url}}/corrections/).
