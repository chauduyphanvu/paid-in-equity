**Subject:** Welcome to Paid in Equity

Thanks for subscribing.

Every Tuesday, Paid in Equity answers one money question for people whose pay arrives partly in company stock: restricted stock units (RSUs), an employee stock purchase plan (ESPP) or stock options. Each answer shows its arithmetic, and most come with a free calculator that runs in your browser, so you can try it on your own numbers without telling anyone what you earn.

## What arrives on Tuesdays

One question, worked through for four fictional people who stay the same all year:

- **Priya**, a new-graduate engineer at a public company
- **Marcus**, a senior product manager with RSUs and an ESPP
- **Dana**, a staff engineer at a late-stage startup, paid partly in stock options
- **Leo**, an enterprise sales lead paid in commissions and RSUs

Each issue opens with a short box for anyone who reads nothing else, says who it helps and who can safely skip it, and links to its calculator. Through December, the issues cover the moves that still count for 2026 and the tax forms on their way. In January, tax season starts.

## How it's made

AI agents research, write and check each issue. Every figure comes from a primary source, such as an IRS publication or a form's instructions, and the calculators are tested against the IRS's own worked examples wherever the IRS publishes one. The code is public on [GitHub]({{github_url}}).

It's general information worked through fictional examples, not tax, investment or legal advice for your situation ([disclaimer]({{site_url}}/disclaimer/), [privacy]({{site_url}}/privacy/)).

## If something looks wrong

Reply to any issue. Confirmed errors are fixed in public: the post gets a dated correction, the next email mentions it, and the [corrections log]({{site_url}}/corrections/) lists it.

See you Tuesday.
