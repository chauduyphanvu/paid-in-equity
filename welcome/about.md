## About page

Paid in Equity answers one money question every Tuesday for people whose pay arrives partly in company stock: restricted stock units (RSUs), an employee stock purchase plan (ESPP) or stock options. It's for anyone at a tech company paid this way: engineers, product managers, designers, recruiters, salespeople and their managers.

Each issue works the question through for four fictional people, Priya, Marcus, Dana and Leo, shows its arithmetic, and usually links to a free calculator that runs in your browser, so you can try it on your own numbers without telling anyone what you earn. Nothing you type leaves the page.

**How it's made.** AI agents research, write and check each issue. Every figure comes from a primary source, such as an IRS publication or a form's instructions, and the calculators are tested against the IRS's own worked examples wherever the IRS publishes one. The code is public on [GitHub]({{github_url}}).

**What it isn't.** General information worked through fictional examples, not tax, investment or legal advice for your situation. Nobody here will tell you what to do with your shares. Read the [disclaimer]({{site_url}}/disclaimer/) and the [privacy policy]({{site_url}}/privacy/).

**Mistakes.** Reply to any issue. Confirmed errors are fixed in public and listed in the [corrections log]({{site_url}}/corrections/).
